# Coverage-Overlap Audit (before paying or renewing)

## Trigger
A purchase, renewal, or new subscription is proposed — or a periodic review of a paid stack — where any part of the value might already be owned, bundled, or covered by another tier.

## Procedure
1. Inventory the paid stack in the domain: every subscription/tier, its monthly cost, and what it actually provides (families, catalogues, features) — from first-party account pages, not marketing copy.
2. Inventory existing ownership in the domain (libraries, licences, bundles included with other tiers).
3. Build the overlap map: for each paid tier, which of its used capabilities are already covered by another tier or by ownership. Dedupe combined lists to unique items before counting anything.
4. Price the scenarios: current stack; lean stack (redundant tiers cut); and any middle option. State monthly and annual figures.
5. Present the redundancy plainly — name the tier, what covers it, and the saving — and leave the cut decision to the owner. A tier is never cancelled by the audit itself.

## Boundary
`financial-planning` covers budgets, affordability, and debt from account data. This audit covers *coverage duplication* across bundles and ownership, which budgeting does not check.

## Evidence
- AI subscriptions, 2026-11-12: consolidation review — $108.99/mo baseline; a membership redundant because a bundle already included its model families; lean scenario ~$708/yr saving.
- Books, 2026-10-24 and 2026-11-21: sale-vs-owned/wishlist validation (owned titles excluded from a BOGO match); combined wishlist deduped to 126 unique items, one wishlist cleaned 48 -> 44 by removing owned titles.
